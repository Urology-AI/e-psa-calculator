Important caveat: 8 of 13 variables (exercise, smoking, diet, race, family history) have negative or near-zero coefficients in this dataset — opposite to clinical expectation. The AUC of 0.707 is driven primarily by age, IPSS mild, and comorbidity score. This is an in-sample result on N=94 with only 23 events and has not been cross-validated. The reversed variables reflect cohort selection bias, not true biology.
